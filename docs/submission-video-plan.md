# Submission video capture plan (PT-07) — for Day to approve

Status: PLAN only. The final video is recorded Nov 4, after the Nov 3
evening freeze. Do not record the final submission video before then.

## Capture route

- Record the real page on Day's Mac as a full-resolution screen
  recording (QuickTime or macOS screen recording), at 3840x2160 if the
  Mac + browser record cleanly at that size, otherwise the highest
  clean resolution. Headless Chrome cannot capture a playing 4K video
  (walking shots in this build were captured from inside the page at
  1x), so the submission video is a screen recording of the live page,
  not a headless capture.
- Browser at full screen, page at /prototype-testing (and the listing
  flow pages below), system notifications off, cursor deliberate.
- One take per segment is fine; edit segments together, keep every
  shown feature real and built during the event.

## What to record, in locked demo order

Opening on the tour kills the demo. Order:

1. Opening line (first line, spoken or on screen): what pre-existed
   vs what was built this week. Draft for Day's wording: "Before this
   week: a phone walkthrough video and listing facts. Built this
   week: everything you are about to see."
2. Raw phone video in: the walkthrough upload.
3. Staged listing out: the generated listing, in minutes.
4. Before/after staging toggle, one room.
5. Grounded copy: claims with their evidence.
6. Comps, labelled demo data.
7. Broker approves: only if it is still in the demo cut. Broker depth
   is v2; do not lead with broker screens.
8. THEN Live-In-It as the second act: /prototype-testing — clip walks
   in at half speed, stops in the living room, furniture cascades from
   the ceiling piece by piece, style picker, Play lifts the pieces
   and walks on.
9. Close on the published page.

## Rules (Discord, Oct 29)

- 3:00 target. Judges stop at 3:00; a few seconds over will not
  disqualify, but plan for 3:00.
- Sped-up real footage is allowed. Simulator / screen recording is fine.
- Understandable in English: voiceover, transcript, or subtitles.
- Show only features built during the event. No slides, no unbuilt ideas.
- Open with one line stating pre-existing vs built-this-week (above).
- Show where Muse Spark / Meta models are used (Use of Meta AI
  Tooling = 25%): name Spark (copy), Wonder (classification),
  Meta Image (staging), SAM 3.1 (masks, floor/window/curtain checks)
  at the moments they appear — a caption or voiceover line each, not
  a architecture lecture.
- Labels stay visible: "Not a real listing — AI-modified" on every
  prototype / generalised page in shot. No real listing facts, no
  keys, no watermarked or unauthorised footage in any frame.
